Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 23, 2026

VeriReceipt ("we," "our," or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information VeriReceipt collects, how it is used, and your choices regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

VeriReceipt is designed with a privacy-first approach. We collect and process the following types of data:

  • Receipt Images: Photos you capture or select from your photo library for scanning. These images are stored locally on your device.
  • Extracted Receipt Data: Merchant names, dates, amounts, line items, categories, and payment methods extracted from your receipts. This data is stored locally on your device.
  • Purchase History: Records of your in-app subscription status, managed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not have access to your Apple ID payment details.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Receipt Analysis: When you scan a receipt, the image is sent to Google's Gemini API for AI-powered data extraction. This is the only time receipt data leaves your device. Google processes the image to extract text and structured data, which is then returned to the app and stored locally.
  • Premium AI Features: If you subscribe to VeriReceipt Premium, additional AI analysis (spending insights, fraud detection, categorization, etc.) also uses Google's Gemini API to process your receipt data.
  • Subscription Management: We use Apple's StoreKit framework to manage subscriptions. All payment processing is handled by Apple.

3. Data Storage

All receipt data, images, and app preferences are stored locally on your device. VeriReceipt does not operate any servers and does not maintain a cloud database of your information. If you delete the app, all locally stored data is permanently removed.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google Gemini API: Receipt images and data are sent to Google's Gemini API for AI analysis. Google's processing of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. We send only the receipt image and minimal context needed for analysis.
  • Apple StoreKit: Subscription purchases and management are handled by Apple and governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • We do not require account creation or login
  • We do not use analytics or crash reporting SDKs
  • We do not use advertising frameworks or ad tracking
  • We do not access your location, contacts, microphone, or health data
  • We do not sell, share, or rent your data to third parties

6. Device Permissions

  • Camera: Used to photograph paper receipts for scanning and data extraction.
  • Photo Library (Read): Used to select existing receipt photos from your library for import.
  • Photo Library (Write): Used to save exported receipt images or reports.

All permissions are optional and requested only when you initiate the relevant action. The app functions with limited capability if permissions are denied.

7. Children's Privacy

VeriReceipt is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data remains on your device for as long as the app is installed. You can delete individual receipts within the app at any time. Uninstalling the app permanently removes all stored data. We have no server-side data to delete because we do not store your information remotely.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
