Common Questions

  • Receipt data: Receipts are stored locally on your device.
  • AI extraction: Receipt images may be sent to Google's Gemini API to extract receipt details.
  • Subscriptions: Premium purchases and cancellations are managed through your Apple ID account settings.
  • Deleting data: Delete individual receipts inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove locally stored data.
